Questions People Ask Us

Ask Us Directly
What is Saphiron Global?

Saphiron Global is a parent company with four businesses: ERP software, export, a technical training center, and a logistics business planned for the future.

Which ERP software is right for my business?

Jasper ERP is for accounting. Obsidian ERP is for garment manufacturing. Ruby ERP is for exporters. Sapphire ERP is for garment accessories and label printing. See all ERP products

Can I see a demo of an ERP product?

Yes, you can ask for one. Send us a message with the product name and your business type, and we will reply. Contact us

Is Ruby ERP a cloud service?

Ruby ERP is a SaaS model software for exporters. Ask us about plans and access.

What products do you export?

We work with jute products, garment products and general products. Our final product range is still being decided, so contact us with what you need. See Export

What can I learn at your training center?

We teach technical skills through NSDA-authorized courses and CBT&A training, and we teach exporting marketing. See Training Center

When will the logistics business start?

It is planned for the future, starting with rent-a-car services. We have no launch date yet. You can ask to be notified. See Logistics

Where can I check your certificates?

Our documents are shown on the Certifications page. Open Certifications
